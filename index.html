Verify our apps

Passport Prime apps from ByteApps are signed with our publisher certificate — byteapps.crt. Before trusting it on your device (Settings > Apps > Allowed Publishers), check that its fingerprint matches:

1bca27c8e765a77fd44922bc058b815b46e627d68f2996e8c38ca6997b6be6f9

Reproduce it yourself — SHA-256 of the certificate's compressed public key:
openssl x509 -in byteapps.crt -pubkey -noout | openssl ec -pubin -conv_form compressed -outform DER | tail -c 33 | shasum -a 256
